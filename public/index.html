How to use

Simple steps for daily use

  1. Clean your scalp. Use a gentle shampoo and dry your scalp completely.
  2. Roll gently. Use the Derma Roller on the thinning areas 1 to 2 times a week. Do not press hard.
  3. Apply Minoxidil. Apply 1 ml of Minoxidil 5% to the dry scalp, twice a day.
  4. Be patient. Keep using it for at least 3 to 4 months before judging results.
