Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Picaso ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, products ordered, and delivery area.
  • Reviews: the name and comment you choose to post on a product page.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser type, collected when you place an order.
  • Advertising cookies: Meta (Facebook) cookies, such as the click identifier that appears when you arrive from a Facebook or Instagram ad.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your order.
  • To contact you by phone about your order.
  • To show product reviews you have chosen to publish.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, using Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversion API.

3. Meta Pixel and Conversion API

We use the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversion API to understand which advertisements lead to orders. These tools send events such as page views, product views, adding to cart, checking out, and purchases to Meta. Your phone number and email are sent only in hashed (scrambled) form. You can control advertising cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook account.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the website and deliver your order, such as delivery partners, and with Meta for the advertising measurement described above. We may also share information when required by law.

5. How long we keep information

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, delivery, and legal obligations. You can ask us to remove a review or other personal information that we no longer need.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. Admin access is protected by a password, and sensitive keys are not shown on the website.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details on our contact page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at our contact page.
