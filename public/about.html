About Picaso

We sell Kirkland Minoxidil 5% and Derma Roller, with cash on delivery across Bangladesh.

Who we are

Picaso is a small online shop based in Dhaka. We started because many people struggle with thinning hair and cannot find clear, honest information about products that work. We sell only the products we are happy to recommend.

What we sell

  • Kirkland Minoxidil 5% Solution: a topical treatment used daily to support hair regrowth.
  • Derma Roller: a microneedle tool that helps the scalp absorb Minoxidil more effectively when used as directed.

How we work

Every order is confirmed by phone before it is shipped. We deliver across Bangladesh, with 70 Tk delivery inside Dhaka and 130 Tk outside Dhaka. Delivery is free when you order 3 or more bottles of Minoxidil.

Important: Results vary from person to person. Hair regrowth usually takes at least 3 to 4 months of consistent daily use. Please read the product instructions carefully, and speak to a doctor if you have a scalp condition or other health concerns before using any hair treatment.
